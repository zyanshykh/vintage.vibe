# Wear your *next find.*

Good clothes, better days. Vintage Vibe is a thrift store where every piece is picked off the pile, one of a kind, and ready to wear again.

## On the rails

Four racks, always changing. Whatever is hanging today may not be here tomorrow.

- ### Jackets

  Light jackets and outer layers for cooler evenings.
- ### Shirts

  Polos, stripes and prints in every colour.
- ### Hoodies

  Zip-ups, pullovers and graphic sweatshirts.
- ### Pants & more

  Cargos, joggers, striped trousers and the odd surprise.

### One of each

Most pieces are single items. If you like it, try it on and take it.

### Always moving

New clothes go on the rails as they come in, so a second visit is worth it.

### Easy to find

Look for the dark sign with the gold VV above the door.
